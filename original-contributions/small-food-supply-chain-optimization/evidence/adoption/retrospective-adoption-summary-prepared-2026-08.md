# Retrospective Adoption and Continued-Use Summary: Small Food Supply Chain Optimization

## Document classification

This retrospective summary was prepared in August 2026 from applicant-provided information. Adoption statements require corroboration with dated organizational evidence.

## Continued use

The full-production solution reportedly remains in use through August 2026 by approximately 76 users or business stakeholders across four sites or facilities.

## Broader adoption

Six additional teams, stores, distribution hubs or business units reportedly adopted the work through:

1. Deployment of the complete solution with configuration changes
2. Reuse of the architecture pattern
3. Retraining of forecasting models using local data

## Standardization

The integrated design reportedly became an approved or published standard supported by a published architecture standard and reusable solution template.

## Evidence identified

- Deployment records
- Later architecture diagrams
- Model-retraining reports
- Published standard
- Reusable template
- Direct-manager confirmation

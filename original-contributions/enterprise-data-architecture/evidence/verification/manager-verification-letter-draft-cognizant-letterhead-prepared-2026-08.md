## Independent Verification of Pallab Haldar's Enterprise Data Architecture Work

I am **Sai Kishore Mallela**, **Senior Technical Architect at Cognizant**. I directly supervised or observed Pallab Haldar's work on an AI-infused Enterprise Data Architecture implemented for a global manufacturing enterprise. My statements are based on professional knowledge and review of relevant project records.

Development began in January 2025, and the architecture entered full production on **February 28, 2025**. It addressed fragmented and siloed data that had produced inconsistent records, delayed disruption response and limited integration of predictive intelligence with procurement, inventory, supplier and production-planning processes.

I can confirm that Mr. Haldar was responsible for the enterprise data architecture, SAP HANA and SAP Datasphere design, SAP Smart Data Integration, predictive-model architecture, governance controls and full-production deployment leadership.

The solution processed approximately **3.5 million records or transactions** from a historical period covering February 2023 through June 2024. It supported demand forecasting, supplier lead-time prediction, material-shortage detection and inventory optimization using LSTM, ensemble-regression and deep-reinforcement-learning approaches.

Based on project records, the final model reported **92.7% forecast accuracy**, **8.4% MAPE**, **1.2-day supplier lead-time MAE** and a **26% reduction in material shortages**. Validation included 10-fold cross-validation and production comparison.

Using the same dataset, target and evaluation definition, baseline forecast accuracy was **92% for traditional statistical forecasting, 88% for rule-based alerts and 82% for manual planning**. The final model therefore improved accuracy by 0.7, 4.7 and 10.7 percentage points, respectively. The 0.7-point improvement over statistical forecasting should be understood as modest; the architecture's wider contribution also included integrated lead-time, shortage and inventory capabilities.

The Enterprise Architecture Lead approved the architecture. As of August 2026, it remained in full-production use, supporting approximately **1,200 users or business stakeholders across four production sites**. Four additional teams, business units or projects adopted the complete architecture with configuration changes or reused its three-tier pattern. The design became an enterprise architecture standard represented by an architecture repository entry and reusable template.

In my professional judgment, Mr. Haldar's contribution went beyond routine implementation. He integrated governed enterprise data, reusable architecture and multiple predictive capabilities into an operational production platform with broad and continuing adoption.

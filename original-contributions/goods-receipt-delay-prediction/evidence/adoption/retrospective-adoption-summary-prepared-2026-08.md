# Retrospective Adoption and Continued-Use Summary: Goods Receipt Delay Prediction

## Document classification

This retrospective summary was prepared in August 2026 from applicant-provided information. It is not an original 2024 adoption record. Each statement should be corroborated with the identified deployment records, architecture documents, screenshots and direct-manager confirmation before formal submission.

## Adoption summary

The Goods Receipt Delay Prediction solution entered limited production during April-June 2024 at a global manufacturing enterprise. The applicant reports that the solution remains in operational use as of August 2026.

## Current operational reach

- Current users or people relying on the solution: Approximately 36
- Current production sites: 4
- Functions previously identified as users: Procurement, production planning, supply-chain operations and supplier management
- Current-use status: Reported as continuing through August 2026

The supporting usage or deployment records should establish whether the stated 36 people are direct application users, prediction recipients, decision-makers relying on outputs or a combination of these groups.

## Reuse beyond the initial deployment

Reuse began during the fourth quarter of 2024, from October through December. Two additional teams or business units reportedly adopted the work through both of the following forms:

1. The same solution was deployed with minor configuration changes.
2. The architecture pattern was reused to build another solution.

This record does not treat configuration-based redeployment and architecture-pattern reuse as identical forms of adoption. The supporting evidence should identify which team or business unit used each form and distinguish any overlap.

## Standardization

The solution architecture reportedly became an approved standard architecture pattern through a published architecture standard issued in July 2024.

The standard's exact title, identifier, version, approving authority and scope were not supplied. These details should be taken directly from the architecture-standard document before external submission.

Because the reported reuse began in the fourth quarter of 2024, after publication of the standard in July 2024, the dated records can help establish a chronological relationship between formal standardization and later reuse. They do not, by themselves, prove that the publication caused the subsequent adoption.

- Deployment records showing the initial and reused implementations

- Direct-manager confirmation of continued use and reuse

Each exhibit should include its original date, owner or issuing authority and public-safe description. Confidential system names, supplier or material identifiers, transaction data, credentials and customer-sensitive information should be redacted.

## Evidence-integrity statement

This summary records reported adoption facts without treating them as independently verified. It distinguishes continued use, solution redeployment, architecture-pattern reuse and formal standardization and does not infer adoption beyond the stated users, sites and additional teams or business units.

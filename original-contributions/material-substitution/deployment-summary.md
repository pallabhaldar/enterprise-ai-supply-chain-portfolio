## Operational problem

Material shortages and limited visibility into acceptable substitute materials complicated procurement and production-planning decisions. Manual evaluation required consideration of material characteristics, historical consumption, procurement information and business constraints.

## Original contribution

Designed an enterprise-data and machine-learning framework for evaluating potential substitute materials. The reported approach combined data preparation, feature engineering, material-similarity analysis, random-forest and decision-tree methods, constraint evaluation and business-rule validation to support governed recommendations.

# Retrospective Adoption and Continued-Use Summary: Inventory Target Prediction

## Document classification

This retrospective summary was prepared in August 2026 from applicant-provided information. It is not an original adoption record. Each claim requires corroboration with dated organizational evidence.

## Continued use

The solution reportedly remains in use as of August 2026 by approximately 34 people across 8 production sites. The evidence should define whether the 34 people are direct users, report recipients, planners relying on model output or a combination of these groups.

## Reuse

Three additional teams or business units reportedly reused the solution or its architecture after the May 2024 deployment. The exact reuse date is unavailable and has not been estimated.

Reuse took two forms:

1. The same solution was deployed with configuration changes.
2. The architecture pattern was reused.

## Standardization

The design reportedly became an approved or published standard architecture pattern supported by:

- A published architecture standard
- A reusable solution template

The exact standard title, identifier, version, publication date and repository record were not supplied.

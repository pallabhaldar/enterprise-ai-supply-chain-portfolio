# Performance Results: Predictive Flight Maintenance

## Evaluation objective

Assess the ability of selected predictive and anomaly-detection methods to identify engine-maintenance risk from sensor history.

## Reported dataset scope

- Engines: 10
- Observation period: 12 months
- Sensor observations: more than 1 million
- Dataset source and authorization: **To be documented**
- Train, validation and test partitions: **To be documented**

## Methods

- Linear regression
- Decision trees
- Logistic regression
- One-Class SVM
- SAP HANA Predictive Analytics Library

## Results required

Add the paper's verified model-by-model metrics, including the target definition, prediction horizon, threshold, confusion matrix where applicable, baseline, test-set size and uncertainty. Do not substitute dataset size for evidence of model performance.

## Operational limitation

Research performance does not establish airworthiness, safety certification, airline deployment, avoided failures, reduced downtime or maintenance-cost savings. Those claims require independent operational evidence.

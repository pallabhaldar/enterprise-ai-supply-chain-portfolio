# Adoption Evidence: Enterprise Data Architecture

## Adopting organization and project

Create one section per verified adopting program. State the organization or authorized anonymized description, project, original dates and applicant's role.

## Deployment level

**To be verified for each project:** Prototype / pilot / limited production / full production / enterprise standard.

## Adoption scope

- Users and teams: **To be verified**
- Sites, regions or business units: **To be verified**
- Systems, models or data products: **To be verified**
- Data volume or transaction scope: **To be verified**
- Duration of use: **To be verified**

## Reuse and influence

Document whether another team reused the modeling standard, replication pattern, migration design, performance method or governed analytics architecture. Identify the adopting team, date, scope and independent evidence.

## Supporting evidence

- Architecture-standard or governance reference
- Production release or operations record
- Usage, training or rollout record
- Reference in another program's architecture
- Signed verifier letter

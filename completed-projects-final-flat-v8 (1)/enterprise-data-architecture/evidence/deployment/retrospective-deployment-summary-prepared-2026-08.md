## Operational problem

Fragmented and siloed enterprise data produced inconsistent records, delayed responses to supply-chain disruptions and prevented predictive models from being embedded consistently in procurement, inventory, supplier and production-planning workflows. The full-production architecture unified operational data and integrated predictive intelligence into those workflows.

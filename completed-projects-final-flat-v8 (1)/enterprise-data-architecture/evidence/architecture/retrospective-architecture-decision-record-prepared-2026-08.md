## Approval

The architecture was formally approved by the Enterprise Architecture Lead before full-production deployment on February 28, 2025.

## Three-tier design

### Data foundation tier

SAP HANA, SAP Datasphere and SAP Smart Data Integration provided governed access to distributed operational data, semantic consistency and analytical processing.

### Intelligence tier

The architecture embedded LSTM demand forecasting, ensemble supplier lead-time regression and deep-reinforcement-learning capabilities for shortage detection and inventory optimization.

### Orchestration and integration tier

Standardized deployment, monitoring, feedback and operational interfaces connected predictions with procurement, inventory, supplier and production-planning workflows.

## Decision rationale

The architecture addressed fragmented data, inconsistent definitions, delayed disruption response and isolated analytical experiments. The approved approach supported governed reuse, reduced integration duplication and placed predictive capabilities within operational processes.

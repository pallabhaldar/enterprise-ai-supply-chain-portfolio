## Independent Verification of Pallab Haldar's Food Supply Chain Optimization Work

I am **Sai Kishore Mallela**, **Senior Technical Architect at Cognizant**. I directly supervised or observed Pallab Haldar's work on an integrated supply-chain optimization solution implemented for a food supply-chain enterprise. My statements are based on professional knowledge and review of relevant project records.

Development began in October 2025, and the solution entered full production in December 2025. It addressed demand volatility, overstocking and stockouts, perishable inventory and spoilage, inefficient delivery routing, supplier variability and high storage, transportation and procurement costs.

I can confirm that Mr. Haldar was responsible for data architecture, forecasting-model design, replenishment and routing optimization, cost-model design, production integration and full-production deployment leadership.

The system used historical data covering February 2022 through January 2025. The precise record count is confidential and is intentionally not disclosed. The production solution combined ARIMA and LSTM forecasting, EOQ-based replenishment, Dijkstra and Vehicle Routing Problem optimization, and linear programming using the Simplex method.

Based on project records, the solution reported **90% weekly demand-forecast accuracy**, **15% lower storage cost**, **20% shorter delivery time** and **12% lower procurement expense**. Validation included 10-fold cross-validation and production comparison.

Using the same dataset and evaluation definition, moving-average forecasting achieved **89.3% accuracy**, while seasonal ARIMA achieved **92.7%**. Seasonal ARIMA therefore exceeded the integrated model's aggregate forecast accuracy. The integrated architecture was selected because it addressed the broader operational problem, supported nonlinear or volatile demand segments and combined forecasting with replenishment, routing and cost optimization. I do not claim that it was the most accurate aggregate forecasting model.

The Technical Review Committee approved the architecture. As of August 2026, it remained in full-production use by approximately **76 users or stakeholders across four facilities**. Six additional teams, stores, distribution hubs or business units adopted the solution through configuration-based deployment, architecture-pattern reuse or local model retraining. The design became a published standard supported by a reusable template.

In my professional judgment, Mr. Haldar's contribution went beyond routine implementation by integrating multiple analytical and optimization methods into a reusable operational solution with continuing and broader adoption.

I provide this statement voluntarily and confirm that it is accurate to the best of my knowledge, subject to Cognizant and client confidentiality requirements.

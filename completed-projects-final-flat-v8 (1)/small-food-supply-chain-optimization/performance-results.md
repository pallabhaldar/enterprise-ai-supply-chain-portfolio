# Performance Results: Small Food Supply Chain Process Optimization

## Evaluation objective

Evaluate whether the combined forecasting, replenishment, routing and cost-optimization framework could improve weekly demand planning and small-grocery supply-chain operations.

## Paper-reported case-study results

| Component | Reported outcome | Current verification status |
|---|---:|---|
| LSTM weekly-demand forecasting | 90% accuracy | Metric definition, dataset and calculation required |
| EOQ replenishment | 15% reduction in storage cost | Baseline period, cost definition and calculation required |
| Route optimization | 20% reduction in delivery time | Route set, comparison period and calculation required |
| Cost minimization | 12% reduction in procurement expense | Expense scope, baseline and calculation required |

These figures are reported in the paper's local-grocery-store case study. The paper does not provide sufficient underlying records to treat them as independently verified operational outcomes.

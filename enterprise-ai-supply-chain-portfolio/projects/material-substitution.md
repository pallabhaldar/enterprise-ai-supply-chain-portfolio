# Strategic Material Substitution

## Project period

2019–2020, subject to confirmation through authorized records.

## Context

The work addressed the challenge of identifying potential material substitutes when procurement constraints, shortages, product requirements, and supply continuity made manual evaluation difficult.

## Personal contribution

Pallab Haldar reports designing an enterprise-data and machine-learning approach that combined material characteristics, historical information, procurement data, and business constraints to support governed substitution recommendations.

## Technical methods

- Enterprise data preparation and feature engineering
- Random-forest and decision-tree methods
- Material similarity and constraint evaluation
- Business-rule validation
- Decision-support integration

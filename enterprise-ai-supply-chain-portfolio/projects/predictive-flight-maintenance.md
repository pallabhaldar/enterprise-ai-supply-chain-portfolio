# Predictive Flight Maintenance

## Objective

Demonstrate an enterprise-data and predictive-analytics framework for detecting maintenance risk from aviation sensor information.

## Reported research scope

- Ten engines
- Twelve months of sensor history
- More than one million sensor observations
- Linear regression, decision trees, logistic regression, and One-Class SVM
- SAP HANA, SDI, Predictive Analytics Library, and SAP Analytics Cloud

## Evidence routes

- IEEE SoutheastCon 2026 publisher record
- Conference program and presentation record
- Architecture diagram
- Reproducible methodology
- Results and limitations
- Source-data provenance and usage authorization

## Important distinction

The repository must clearly distinguish a research demonstration from an operational airline production deployment unless production deployment is independently verified.

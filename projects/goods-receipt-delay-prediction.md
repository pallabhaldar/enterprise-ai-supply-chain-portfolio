# Goods-Receipt Delay Prediction

## Objective

Predict delayed goods-receipt events early enough to support procurement and supply-planning intervention.

## Reported technical scope

- Approximately three years of ERP history
- Approximately 250,000 goods-receipt events
- Six-month validation period
- Random-forest regression
- SAP HANA Predictive Analytics Library deployment context

## Reported result

An approximately 81% result has been reported. The final evidence must identify whether this represents accuracy, R², forecast accuracy, or another metric, and must include the baseline and validation protocol.
